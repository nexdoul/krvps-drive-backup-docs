Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026. KRVPS Drive Backup is a personal utility operated by the Google account owner.

Data and permissions

The utility uses Google Drive's drive.file permission to create and manage its backup files. It accesses file contents and metadata to upload, verify, retain, download, and restore backups. It may read account identity and storage quota information to confirm that it is connected to the intended account and has available storage.

Storage and use

Backup data is stored on the owner's server and in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in restricted files on owner-controlled computers. This website does not receive credentials or backup contents.

Sharing

The utility does not sell personal information, serve advertising, or share Google user data with other parties for their independent purposes. Google processes files as the selected storage provider. Google user data is not used to develop or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Retention and control

The owner configures backup retention and can delete backup files or revoke this app's access from Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access; the owner can separately delete existing local and Drive backups.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the account owner or the support email shown on the app's Google authorization screen.

The utility's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
